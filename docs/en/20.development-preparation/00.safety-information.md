Before developing payload devices with PSDK, please carefully read and comply with the following safety information to avoid personal injury or damage to equipment or property caused by improper operation.

## Firmware Upgrade
Before developing payload devices, you need to first use <a href="https://www.dji.com/cn/downloads/softwares/assistant-dji-2-for-matrice" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DJI Assistant 2</a> to upgrade the firmware of the aircraft and the development kit/hardware platform. For detailed descriptions of firmware versions, please refer to the <a href="../" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Release Notes</a>.

To ensure the stable operation of DJI aircraft and remote controllers and reduce the accident rate, DJI strongly recommends that you actively encourage your customers to upgrade to the latest aircraft firmware whenever new firmware is released.


## Development and Testing
When developing payload devices with PSDK, to protect developers from accidents, please note the following:

* When developing payload devices with PSDK or testing payload devices developed based on PSDK, please remove the propellers from the aircraft.
* Do not approach the aircraft motors while they are rotating.
* Do not input high-power current into the aircraft power output port.

### Key Function Development

To facilitate version maintenance, problem localization, and after-sales support for payload devices, DJI recommends that when developing payload functions with Payload SDK, you complete the development of the following key functions in combination with your business scenarios:

* <a href="../function-set/access-authentication.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Access and Authentication</a>: The basic prerequisite for a payload to access the flight platform. Only after completing secure access and identity authentication can the remaining functions be used.
* <a href="../function-set/bind.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Binding Status Notification</a>: After the device accesses the MQTT broker, monitor the binding status and guide the pilot to complete binding, ensuring that business requests are executed after the device is authorized.
* <a href="../function-set/aircraft-information.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Aircraft Information</a>: Obtain the model, firmware version, mobile App information, and onboard device inventory of the current flight platform, to facilitate recording the operating environment and assist with problem analysis.
* <a href="../function-set/local-update.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Local Upgrade</a>: Supports the payload firmware upgrade capability, facilitating timely problem fixes and guiding customers to upgrade.

### Flight Without Remote Controller

* When developing and using the function of flight without a remote controller, please fully understand and strictly comply with local regulations, and bear the relevant regulatory risks brought by flying without a remote controller.
* For countries or regions that enforce Remote ID regulations and require real-time broadcasting of the pilot's position, developers should call the corresponding PSDK interfaces to report the true and precise pilot position in real time, ensuring flight compliance. If developers fail to report the pilot position as required, the aircraft may be blocked from flying, for example when the flight region is the United States or the aircraft version is the US version.
* When using the function of flight without a remote controller, there is a conflict with the DJI Care fly-away protection benefit. If the aircraft has already been bound to a remote controller, it will be unable to take off in the state of operating without a remote controller. If this restriction is unacceptable, you can, in the flight-without-remote-controller mode, not bind the aircraft and the remote controller. (If the aircraft has already been bound to a remote controller, perform an unbinding operation.) It is recommended to explain this restriction and the related rights and responsibilities to third-party developers and end users.
